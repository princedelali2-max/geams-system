Your people

Backup of your data

A file with your crew list and every alert forwarded to your crews, encrypted with a passphrase only you know. GEAMS cannot recover the passphrase: if it is lost, the file cannot be opened. Keep the file somewhere safe off this device. This does not include police records.

To open it: node tools/open-agency-backup.js file.enc (in the GEAMS folder) and type the passphrase.

Automatic activity log

Written by the system, not typed by anyone: every alert received, crew accepted, en route, on scene and call ended (with the outcome and note), last 48 hours. Times are the server's.
