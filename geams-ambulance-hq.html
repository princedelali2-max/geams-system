🏥 Patient transfer: hospital to hospital

Collect a patient at one hospital and deliver them to another. The crew's app guides them to the pickup, then, once the patient is on board, to the destination. En route status and ETA come from the crew's live GPS and update every few seconds.

GEAMS does not contact either hospital. Call them to confirm the bed and the handover. Hospital positions come from the map search or from a point you set yourself.

Create an alert for your crews

Sends a new alert straight to one station's ambulance crews, without going through a control room. Use it for something your HQ learns of directly. The control room does not get it on its own screen.

Message your crews

Backup of your data

A file with your crew list and every alert forwarded to your crews, encrypted with a passphrase only you know. GEAMS cannot recover the passphrase: if it is lost, the file cannot be opened. Keep the file somewhere safe off this device. This does not include police records.

To open it: node tools/open-agency-backup.js file.enc (in the GEAMS folder) and type the passphrase.
